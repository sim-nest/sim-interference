# Project animation frames without re-solving

Solve one phasor field, then project three instantaneous scalar frames at
quarter-cycle clock positions. Animation changes only the observation clock;
the physical propagation, field storage, and sampling certificate remain
unchanged.

The frames show a homogeneous, isotropic, three-dimensional scalar free field,
without polarization, impedance, interfaces, obstacles, or diffraction.
Amplitude-squared frames would be normalized observables, not physical
intensity, power, or energy.
