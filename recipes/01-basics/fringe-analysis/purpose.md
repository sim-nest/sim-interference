# Build and analyze a certified standing wave

Construct a bounded counter-propagating plane-wave scenario, solve it on a
strictly admitted physical plane, and derive a fringe report. The report keeps
the solve's sampling certificate and the projection identity beside its
statistics, local node/antinode candidates, and Michelson contrast.

The same recipe constructs a strict rectangular point-element aperture to show
that its requested total amplitude is divided across elements and that both
neighbour spacings are reported in wavelengths.
