# Reduce every source cell through a detector

Solve a constant forward-plane field on an odd 3-by-5 grid and reduce it to a
2-by-2 scalar detector. The deterministic partition includes every source cell
exactly once even though neither axis divides evenly, and the certificate
reports the resulting footprint range and information loss.

The detector observes a homogeneous, isotropic, three-dimensional scalar
free-field. Its amplitude-squared rule is a normalized area mean, not
impedance-derived physical intensity, power, or energy; polarization,
interfaces, obstacles, and diffraction remain outside scope.
