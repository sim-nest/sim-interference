# Follow an outgoing travelling wave

Evaluate one forward-plane emitter at quarter-wavelength intervals. The checked
phasors rotate in the positive spatial direction because the model pairs
`exp(+i k s)` propagation with the `exp(-i omega t)` time convention.

The emitter is one scalar free-field component in a homogeneous medium. The
recipe does not model an interface, boundary reflection, or polarization.
